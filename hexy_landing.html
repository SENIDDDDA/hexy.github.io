/01 — About Us

Forged in the
North. Built for
the battlefield.

Hexy is a Norwegian defense technology company building modular unmanned systems for the new era of European security. We exist because Europe can no longer afford to depend on foreign supply chains, foreign airframes, or single-mission drones that become inventory dead-weight the moment the mission shifts.

The team is multinational and master's-trained, drawn from Norway, France, Romania, Bosnia & Herzegovina, Syria, and Vietnam — robotics engineers, electronics designers, and business operators with one shared conviction: the next generation of defense hardware must be configurable, sovereign, and operator-friendly.

We work directly with Norwegian defense stakeholders. We test at Area 67. We design for Forsvarsmateriell procurement criteria from day one — lower lifecycle cost, faster delivery, allied interoperability, and Norwegian security of supply.

/03.2 — MODULARITY ARCHITECTURE

Four pillars,
one airframe.

Structure

Switch between 4, 6 and 8 rotor arms. Field-swap the mainframe, top cap, motor arms, and support legs without tools.

Payloads

Standardized attachment ports accept LiDAR, EO/IR cameras, navigation sensors, EW antennas, datalinks, and laser designators.

Propulsion

Electric for low-signature ISR. Combustion for endurance. Hybrid for combined dwell and stealth profiles.

Comms

Plug-and-play SATCOM, LTE and line-of-sight datalinks. Mesh networking for swarm or relay operations.

/04 — Go-To-Market

Norway first.
Then NATO.

Defense procurement is relationship-driven, multi-year, alliance-linked. We're not chasing volume — we're earning a domestic reference customer.

Our buyer is the central state. In Norway, that's the Norwegian Defence Materiel Agency (NDMA / Forsvarsmateriell). We qualify on DOFFIN, respond to RFIs, run pilots with Forsvaret, and only then leverage Norwegian credibility into Nordic and broader NATO markets. Rushing international expansion without a domestic win would weaken everything that follows.
